Bug Report App

Effective Date: June 8, 2025  ·  Version: 1.0  ·  Developer: Da'Limbo

Overview

Bug Report is an educational app that helps you understand how mobile apps access device data. It visualizes common data permissions and shows you what information apps on your phone may be collecting — all processed locally on your device.

Your Data, Your Device

All data collected by Bug Report stays on your device. We do not send your personal data to our servers or any third parties. You control what is collected and can delete it at any time.

  • No account or login required
  • No cloud upload — your data never leaves your phone
  • Offline support — Bug Report functions without internet access
  • User control — you can clear, export, or disable data access at any time from app settings

What Data Is Accessed

Bug Report may access the following categories of data only with your explicit permission. All accessed data is used only for local analysis and remains on your device:

  • Device Info — to show how apps fingerprint your device
  • App Usage / Installed Apps — to visualize behavior-based targeting
  • Advertising ID — to illustrate ad personalization practices
  • Location — to demonstrate how apps use geolocation
  • Contacts — to show how metadata from contacts may be accessed
  • Clipboard Content — to help detect clipboard sniffing practices
  • Motion/Sensor Data — to demonstrate use of sensors for analytics or tracking
  • Network Info (IP/Wi-Fi) — to show how apps may use network data for profiling

Advertising & Personalization

Bug Report displays ads using Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These ad networks may access certain identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) to serve personalized or contextual ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

You can disable ad personalization in the Bug Report app settings or from your device's system ad preferences. We do not knowingly sell or share your personal data.

Exporting & Clearing Data

All export and wipe features are available offline. You can:

  • Export logs and diagnostics in JSON or CSV
  • Clear all collected data instantly via app settings

Your Privacy Rights

  • Right to Access — export all data the app has accessed
  • Right to Delete — clear all stored logs and signal data at any time
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — disable permissions from within the app or device settings
  • No Automated Profiling — Bug Report does not build a persistent profile or track across apps

If you are a resident of the EEA, United Kingdom, or a region with similar privacy laws (e.g., California CCPA), you have specific rights regarding your personal data.

Children

The app is not for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

GameSure Game

Effective Date: August 28, 2025  ·  Last Updated: August 28, 2025  ·  Developer: Da'Limbo

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Da'Limbo ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use GameSure. By installing or using GameSure, you agree to this policy.

What We Collect

We design GameSure to collect as little personal information as possible. We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, phone number, or address to play.

Depending on your device and the features you use, we or our service providers may process:

  • Device & App Data — device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID), device model, OS version, app version, language, country/region, time zone, IP address, crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data
  • Gameplay Data — non-personal gameplay events (e.g., level progress, session length, score milestones)
  • Purchases (if IAPs are enabled) — purchase status and receipts; no full payment card details are processed by us — purchases are handled by Google Play

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data.

How We Use Information

  • Operate and improve the game (performance, stability, bug fixing)
  • Analytics (e.g., which levels are hardest; where players churn)
  • Advertising (to show ads, cap frequency, and fight fraud)
  • In-app purchases (to fulfill transactions and restore purchases)
  • Security (abuse and fraud prevention, service integrity)

Third-Party Services

We use standard mobile SDKs. These providers may collect or receive information described above under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Ads — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Legal Bases (EEA/UK Users)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we process on the basis of:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the game you installed
  • Legitimate interests — analytics, security, crash reporting
  • Consent — personalized advertising (managed by Google's consent tools where required)

You can withdraw or change consent for personalized ads via your device's ad settings or the consent prompt where shown.

Ads & Your Choices

GameSure may display ads. You can opt out of ad personalization on Android via Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (wording may vary). You may also reset your Advertising ID in the same settings.

We do not sell personal information as defined by CCPA.

Permissions Used

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — load ads, analytics, and online content
  • BILLING — in-app purchases
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) — optional gameplay notifications
  • WAKE_LOCK / VIBRATE — keep screen on during play; haptic feedback

Data Retention

Analytics and diagnostics are retained by our providers for the minimum period needed for trend analysis, debugging, and legal obligations (typically 2–26 months depending on provider settings). Purchase records are kept as required for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, and chargeback handling.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information (e.g., transport encryption, access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

International Transfers

Information may be processed on servers outside your country. Where required, our providers implement appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to, or restrict processing, and data portability. Contact us to exercise your rights — we may need to verify your request.

Children's Privacy

GameSure is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new effective date. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice where required.
